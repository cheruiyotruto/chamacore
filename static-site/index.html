The real problem

Group funds do not fail because members do not care. They fail because the records are hard to trust.

When contributions, loans and payouts live in different books, even a careful committee spends its time reconciling instead of serving members.

Scattered records

Notebooks, spreadsheets and phone messages never agree on the same balance.

Late contributions

Members forget due dates and officials chase payments without a clear list.

Unclear approvals

Loan and payout decisions are hard to audit when authority lives in inboxes.

Slow reports

Every meeting needs a fresh summary assembled by hand from several sources.

The answer is not more spreadsheets. It is one ledger, one member view and one set of rules that everyone can see.

One connected system

From contribution to decision, everything stays connected.
