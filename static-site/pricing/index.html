Good to know

Pricing questions, answered.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Your plan can be changed as your membership grows or your group needs more modules. Ask us about the best time to switch.

Do members pay separately?

No. Members join the workspace created by their group. There is no separate member licence to buy.

Can we pay annually?

Yes. Annual billing can be arranged. Request access and tell us which plan you prefer.

What happens during setup?

We confirm your roles, products, charges, reminders and payment details before members are invited.
